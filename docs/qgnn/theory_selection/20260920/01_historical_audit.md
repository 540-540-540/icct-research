## 1. Earlier graph/PQC/QGAT generations
Sources: prediction/quantum.py; experiments/qgat_factorial/graph.py; experiments/qgat_value/graph.py; reports/qgat_formal/summary.json; reports/qgat_factorial_full/seed2026/{protocol,summary}.json; reports/qgnn_bottleneck/bdx01_20260914_01/summary.json.
The early global graph used two role qubits per car, physical edge-conditioned ZX rotations and local mixing. The later QGAT used a neighbor-index register and query-key overlaps controlling value-state amplitudes, with measurable mixed/coherent Pauli readouts and postselection probabilities. Adding relation-conditioned score/value rotations preserves physical information but does not by itself model neighbor-neighbor relations. Other edge-PQC variants retained substantial classical message/value/update computation.
These runs used legacy f01d data, SNR 5/10/15/20, 5549 training origins, different GPT-2 and frontend contracts: they are NOT SinD experiments. Three legacy QGAT seeds underperformed their classical graph controls. In the 1/2 state-qubit x all/45m-neighbor factorial, two qubits alone did not improve performance; the combined locality/two-qubit cell helped relative to the one-qubit all-neighbor cell. Encoding and readout also changed, so this is not a pure qubit-count intervention.
Legacy bottleneck audit found highly correlated Q/G prediction errors, later train improvement with validation degradation, and largely unchanged inputs across SNRs due to the old input pipeline. None of these frontend conclusions can be transferred to the present continuous SinD sensing cache.
Reusable: physical edge content; algebraic circuit/reference equivalence; proper mask/gradient checks. Reject: stronger Hilbert-space rhetoric, edge-PQC decoration, and assuming more qubits or longer training is automatically beneficial.

## 2. RC-HQGNN
Source: docs/qgnn/FINAL_QGNN_ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_20260919.md and prediction/qgnn_final/.
Four independent eight-qubit channels, three reupload rounds, joint Z/ZZ/ZZZ phases with noncommuting local rotations. Shared per-car GRU32; pair16 and triplet32 summaries. Measured X/Y/Z, connected K2/K3 and quantum-correlation-weighted physical relation values feed a per-node 64D representation.
Full SinD 15802/1880, 0dB, seed2026, 20 epochs: Q ADE/FDE=0.51094460/1.12605076; adaptive classical higher-order=0.49146503/1.07928883. Q is worse by 3.96/4.33%. In the predefined 140-window strong-closing stratum, Q gains 4.29/6.35%; low-closing windows deteriorate. Removing couplings post training worsens predictions, demonstrating reliance, not quantum-specific advantage.
Increasing phase/correlation strength did not solve the task. Relation-carrying readout improved over a weaker quantum version but not the classical control. K3 is a connected measurement cumulant, not an entanglement witness.
A separate representation probe against a classical residual reported R2 about .922 before compression and .919 for final local+quantum64D: catastrophic angle/64D compression is NOT established as the sole cause. This surrogate probe is not proof of future sufficiency.

## 4. Weighted Multi-j Raj-style Candidate A
Sources: QGNN_SELECTION_CANDIDATE_A_RAJ_FREEZE_20260919.md; ROUND4_RAJ_PAPER_NATIVE_FINDINGS_20260919.md; ROUND4_RAJ_FULLTRAIN_RESULTS_20260919.json; actual raj_subset.py and raj_paper.py.
Both j=2 and j=3 run for every scene. State-like arrays have C(8,j) subset rows and C(6,3)=20 embedding amplitudes. Learned physical edge weights drive exp(iH) on the Johnson subset graph; a Cayley/compound embedding operation mixes embedding coordinates. Additive feature reupload and per-row normalization follow. Readout pools real/imaginary amplitudes from four depth snapshots through subset incidence and adds a local-history residual.
4096 windows, 20 epochs, seed2026: Q .516457940/1.102781152 vs Johnson .525952385/1.129781810. Seed2027: Q .530895042/1.148233937 vs Johnson .550422603/1.189891210. Ratios of two-seed mean errors give gains 2.696% ADE and 2.960% FDE.
Full15802, same seed2026: Q .481258665/1.051172996 vs Johnson .482314337/1.049728396. ADE gain .219%, FDE - .138%, J gain .033%; both best at epoch9. It is a near tie, not a scalable confirmed quantum win.
### Additional code-audit qualifications
The primary Johnson comparator has a FIXED binary subset adjacency, whereas the quantum candidate has physical graph-conditioned weighted hopping. This is a functional confound despite shared raw inputs. A later weighted classical diagnostic used a different single-j/no-local/RDM-related interface and does not close the original multi-j comparison.
The successful candidate directly consumes real/imaginary amplitudes; these are not a Born observable and depend on a phase convention. Add-and-row-normalize is not a deterministic unitary channel on a generic unknown state. The model contains genuine quantum-inspired unitary dynamics, but a complete measurement/state-preparation implementation for the entire predictor has not been specified. Its simulation results remain valid as algorithmic evidence, not an end-to-end hardware-ready QGNN proof.
### Why small-data gains can vanish
Hypothesis: constrained norm-preserving, shared-parameter dynamics provide useful regularization with fewer observations, while the classical model catches up when information/exposure increases. Counter-hypotheses: learned physical edge weighting; optimization trajectories; subset selection; local bypass and amplitude readout; common decoder ceiling; chance plus correlated validation windows.
Crucial confound: 20 epochs means 2560 updates at4096/B32 but9880 updates at15802/B32. Small-data best epochs19/20 are boundary-limited; full-data best9 is about4446 updates. Thus existing results do NOT isolate sample complexity from optimization exposure. Seed2027 also changes the selected4096 subset, not just initialization. Need nested data-size x update-budget curves, fixed subset seeds vs model seeds, same physical weighting, and a complex/unitary classical control before attributing small-data advantage to quantum sample efficiency.

## 6. SQM-GNN Candidate B
Sources: QGNN_SELECTION_CANDIDATE_B_SQM_GNN_INITIAL_AUDIT_20260919.md; SQM_GNN_UMSG_SOURCE_BOUNDARY_20260919.md.
This is only a source-level candidate audit, not a trained ICCT model. High-dimensional temporal SinD information needs an explicit new encoding design. Parameter sharing alone does not prove invariance of an ordered product of noncommuting center-neighbor gates. Official reusable implementation and exact ansatz need external verification. Wireless scalar power-control gains do not establish trajectory gains.

## Audit outcome
Retain mechanisms: explicit subset identity; physical graph conditioning; bounded context; norm-preserving joint dynamics; temporal order; measurable structured readout; own-history preservation with no classical cross-agent bypass. Reject unsupported narratives: entanglement implies advantage; WL guarantees continuous-trajectory superiority; hardware access to amplitudes is free; same epochs isolate data efficiency; correlation is causality; a negative post-hoc intervention rules out a training mechanism; all historical runs used SinD.
Final architecture is intentionally not selected in this stage. It will be chosen after external mechanism review.
